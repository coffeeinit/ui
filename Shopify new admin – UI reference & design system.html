Design principles (from Shopify’s announcement)

  • Focus – a page should say what matters on arrival.
  • Directness – the object itself becomes the path to action.
  • De-layering – fewer containers; structure does the work.
  • Personality – character, motion and polish where they add delight (the 3D objects, gradients).
  • Built for what comes next – room for Sidekick, floating or in a side panel.

Research notes: why it feels easy and premium

  • Search, notifications and store picker move into the sidebar, so settings feel anchored, not like a separate app.
  • Single dark action per view (pill) against pale chips gives an unambiguous primary.
  • Almost no borders or shadows; whitespace and soft radii carry hierarchy.
  • Sidekick is always present at the bottom but never forced; escalates from a floating bar to a side panel.
  • Colour is rationed: neutral UI, with yellow and green only for status. (These notes are my interpretation, not Shopify statements.)
